# Bio.

Dr. Huang currently serves as an **Assistant Professor** at the [***Advanced Institute of Convergence Knowledge (So-Go-Chi) Informatics***](https://www.aisogochi.tohoku.ac.jp/), [***Tohoku University***](https://www.tohoku.ac.jp/en/) (since April 2025), and also holds a concurrent appointment at the [***Tohoku University Cognitive Neuroscience Application Center (CogNAC)***](https://cognac.tohoku.ac.jp/eng/member/member.php?d=K8lIzU3Ny7c1N1YrSi0pLcqLL8gosE0MN82OikgvTXbPKAcA). He received his **Ph.D.** from Tohoku University in March 2025 after completing his doctoral research in the [***IIC Lab***](http://www.iic.ecei.tohoku.ac.jp/index.html) under the joint supervision of [**Prof. Shinichiro OMACHI**](http://www.iic.ecei.tohoku.ac.jp/~machi/index-j.html) and [**Prof. Tomo MIYAZAKI**](https://tomomiyazaki.github.io/). Prior to his current appointment, he attained his **B.Eng. and M.Eng. degrees** from the [*Guilin University of Electronic Technology*](https://www.guet.edu.cn/) in 2018 and 2021, respectively, under the supervision of [**Prof. Zetao JIANG**](https://orcid.org/0000-0002-0914-2131). During his doctoral studies, he was honored to be a [**JSPS Research Fellow (日本学術振興会特別研究員 DC2, Research Fellowship for Young Scientists)**](https://www.jsps.go.jp/english/e-pd/index.html). He also conducted research at [***Harvard Medical School***](https://hms.harvard.edu/), working with [**Prof. Jinsong OUYANG**](https://medicine.yale.edu/profile/jinsong-ouyang/) and [**Prof. Xiaofeng LIU**](https://medicine.yale.edu/profile/xiaofeng-liu/), and at [***National Taiwan University***](https://www.ntu.edu.tw/english/), collaborating with [**Prof. Ai-Chun PANG**](https://www.csie.ntu.edu.tw/~acpang/). His research focuses on **computer vision and deep learning**, with particular interests in **image processing, data science, and pattern recognition**. His work has been recognized with the [**RIEC Award**](https://www.riec.tohoku.ac.jp/ja/info/riec-award/r7/) and the **ECEI Excellent Young Researcher Award (若手優秀研究賞)**.
